# Warmup Cosine Scheduler

<NotationProvider>

<NotationSwitch />

## The warmup

During warmup the learning rate increases linearly from a
small value to the target peak over a set number of steps:

<Equation
  code={String.raw`\color{#f59e0b} \text{lr} = \text{warmup\_start\_lr} + \text{epoch} \cdot \frac{\text{base\_lr} - \text{warmup\_start\_lr}}{\text{warmup\_epochs} - 1}`}
  math={String.raw`\Large \color{#f59e0b} \eta_t = \eta_{\text{start}} + t \cdot \frac{\eta_{\text{peak}} - \eta_{\text{start}}}{T_{\text{warmup}} - 1}`}
/>

## The cosine decay

After warmup, the learning rate follows a cosine curve from the peak
down to a minimum value:

<Equation
  code={String.raw`\color{#6366f1} \text{lr} = \text{min\_lr} + \tfrac{1}{2}(\text{base\_lr} - \text{min\_lr}) \left(1 + \cos\!\left(\pi \cdot \frac{\text{epoch} - \text{warmup\_epochs}}{\text{max\_epochs} - \text{warmup\_epochs}}\right)\right)`}
  math={String.raw`\Large \color{#6366f1} \eta_t = \eta_{\min} + \tfrac{1}{2}(\eta_{\text{peak}} - \eta_{\min}) \left(1 + \cos\!\left(\pi \cdot \frac{t - T_{\text{warmup}}}{T_{\max} - T_{\text{warmup}}}\right)\right)`}
/>

Why cosine and not linear? Cosine keeps the learning rate near the peak
for longer in the middle of training (where most useful learning
happens), then accelerates the decay toward the end. The initial descent
is gentle, the final descent is steep — and the transition is smooth,
with no discontinuities.

## The full schedule

Combining both phases:

<Equation
  code={String.raw`\text{lr} = \begin{cases} \color{#f59e0b} \text{warmup\_start\_lr} + \text{epoch} \cdot \dfrac{\text{base\_lr} - \text{warmup\_start\_lr}}{\text{warmup\_epochs} - 1} & \color{#f59e0b} \text{epoch} < \text{warmup\_epochs} \\[12pt] \color{#6366f1} \text{min\_lr} + \tfrac{1}{2}(\text{base\_lr} - \text{min\_lr})\!\left(1 + \cos\!\left(\pi \cdot \dfrac{\text{epoch} - \text{warmup\_epochs}}{\text{max\_epochs} - \text{warmup\_epochs}}\right)\right) & \color{#6366f1} \text{otherwise} \end{cases}`}
  math={String.raw`\Large \eta_t = \begin{cases} \color{#f59e0b} \eta_{\text{start}} + t \cdot \dfrac{\eta_{\text{peak}} - \eta_{\text{start}}}{T_{\text{warmup}} - 1} & \color{#f59e0b} t < T_{\text{warmup}} \\[12pt] \color{#6366f1} \eta_{\min} + \tfrac{1}{2}(\eta_{\text{peak}} - \eta_{\min})\!\left(1 + \cos\!\left(\pi \cdot \dfrac{t - T_{\text{warmup}}}{T_{\max} - T_{\text{warmup}}}\right)\right) & \color{#6366f1} \text{otherwise} \end{cases}`}
/>
