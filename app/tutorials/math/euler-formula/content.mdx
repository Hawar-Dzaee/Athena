# Euler's Formula

Euler's formula connects the exponential function to trigonometry:

$$
e^{i\theta} = \cos\theta + i\sin\theta
$$

The real part is $\cos\theta$ and the imaginary part is $\sin\theta$.
As $\theta$ sweeps from $0$ to $2\pi$, the point $e^{i\theta}$ traces the **unit circle** in the complex plane.

## Explore it

Pick a formula — $e^{i\theta}$, $e^{2\pi i\theta}$, $e^{2i\theta}$, or $e^{3i\theta}$ — and set $\theta$ with the slider.
The **real** and **imaginary** parts update numerically. Hit **Play** to sweep $\theta$ across its full range and watch the circle(s) form.

Higher coefficients wind the point around the circle multiple times; $e^{2\pi i\theta}$ completes exactly one revolution as $\theta$ goes from $0$ to $1$.
